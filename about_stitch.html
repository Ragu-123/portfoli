Technical Background

Emerging from a dual background in Computational Mathematics and Cognitive Science, my journey into Artificial Intelligence began at the intersection of formal logic and pattern recognition. As a fresh graduate, I have spent the last four years deconstructing the underlying mechanics of deep learning.

My focus lies in the optimization of Large Language Models (LLMs) and the development of robust neural architectures that prioritize both efficiency and explainability. My work aims to move beyond "black-box" models towards systems that reflect clear, interpretable structural integrity.

Research Philosophy

  • 01

    Computational Humility

    Prioritizing algorithmic efficiency and resource-conscious design over brute-force scaling. Smaller, smarter, and more specific models.

  • 02

    Structural Transparency

    Designing with interpretability as a first-class citizen. If a model's logic cannot be traced, its utility is inherently limited in critical domains.

  • 03

    Iterative Rigor

    Applying a scholarly approach to debugging and optimization, treating every failure as a high-fidelity data point for the next iteration.
